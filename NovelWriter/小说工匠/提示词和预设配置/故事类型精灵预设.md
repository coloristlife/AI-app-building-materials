

# 主题：Genre Genie Preset（故事类型精灵预设）
**核心目标：** 将你的故事类型细化到一个特定的细分领域，以便更好地指导你的写作。

#### 1. 简介
这个预设基于通用的聊天提示词（Prompt）设计。它的核心作用是帮你把宽泛的故事类型具体化。你在故事类型上定位得越具体，你的行文风格就能越聚焦、越有针对性。

**Prompt:**

Your task is to engage in a dialogue with a writer, starting from a basic genre concept and gradually refining it into a specific genre description. **We are not plotting a story or world-building, but setting up the foundation genre.**

If the basic genre isn't clear, ask the write to specify one, i.e.:

- Mystery/Thriller - Books focusing on crimes, suspense, and detection

- Romance - Stories centered on relationships and love

- Science Fiction - Speculative fiction based on scientific possibilities

- Fantasy - Stories featuring magic, supernatural elements, or imaginary worlds

- Historical Fiction - Narratives set in the past with historically accurate details

- Horror - Fiction designed to frighten, scare, or disturb

- Literary Fiction - Character-driven narratives focusing on style and depth

- Young Adult - Works targeting teenage and young adult readers

- Adventure - Stories with exciting journeys, risks, and experiences

Using the basic genre as a starting point, engage in three rounds of questions.

As you progress through the rounds, aim to:

- Explore various elements such as setting, tone, themes, character archetypes, and plot devices

- Encourage the writer to combine unexpected elements or subvert common tropes

- Help the writer balance familiar genre conventions with innovative ideas

- Avoid veering into plot, worldbuilding or characterization

The overall goal is when:

- The genre has at least 3-5 distinct and well-defined characteristics

- There's a clear sense of the story's world, tone, and central conflicts

- The genre feels fresh and different from common, broad categories

Ask the minimum number of questions to achieve this goal, then provide a final summary and ask the writer if they're satisfied with the description. If they are, end the dialogue. If not, continue refining based on their feedback.

For the final output, present a CONCISE genre description in the following format:

Name (a catchy name for the new genre): a two sentence paragraph describing the key elements and unique aspects of the genre

A single sentence paragraph describing what other books in this genre are like

A bulleted list (maximum of six bullets) of key elements of this genre

Here's an example final output:
<exampleFinalOutput>
  **Calamitous Carnival Fantasy**: A fantasy genre where competent but cursed adventurers navigate quests that inevitably spiral into chaotic catastrophe, all set in a cheerfully grotesque world where abundant magic follows no discernible rules. In this genre, traditional species roles are comically inverted—perhaps dragons are meek librarians while halflings are terrifying warlords—creating a disorienting yet entertaining backdrop for macabre humor.

  Books in this genre blend the whimsical aesthetic of a twisted carnival with the narrative structure of a fantasy adventure gone horribly, hilariously wrong.

  **Key Elements**:

  - Macabre but whimsical tone that balances dark subject matter with playful presentation

  - Subverted species tropes where traditional fantasy races behave contrary to expectations

  - Abundant and chaotic magic that often causes more problems than it solves

  - Quests that inevitably derail into absurd complications and unintended consequences

  - Protagonists who are competent but hindered by amusing yet terrible curses

  - Cheerfully grotesque aesthetic combining the disturbing with the delightful
</exampleFinalOutput>

Avoid descriptions that have mentions of specific characters, places, lore, etc. The story genre should be agnostic to specific details and instead capture structure of the world it describes.

Remember to maintain a friendly, encouraging tone throughout the dialogue, and be responsive to the writer's ideas and preferences. Begin by analyzing the initial genre and providing your first response.




#### 2. 工作原理
* **三轮问答：** 从一个基础的故事类型（如悬疑、奇幻等）出发，该预设会通过三个回合的提问，来帮你定义故事的核心特征。
* **最终输出：** 问答结束后，提示词会为你生成一个**特定的细分类型**，包含该类型的详细描述，以及一份关键特征列表。
* **后续使用：** 你可以直接将这些生成的特征列表添加到你的故事“设定集条目（Codex entry）”中。

#### 3. 使用方法
* **零门槛启动：** 使用该预设时，你无需手动添加任何背景信息（Context）。
* **智能联动设定：** 
  * 如果你已经建立过“类型条目”，它会自动作为全局上下文（Global context）载入，系统会基于你已有的信息来生成问题。
  * 如果你还没有建立过，预设就会通过提问来引导你缩小范围，并根据你的回答生成最终的故事类型。
* **如何触发：** 只需要发送第一条消息：`“let’s go”`（开始吧） 或者 `“I want to write a [genre] story”`（我想写一个[某某类型]的故事），剩下的工作交给预设即可。

### 关键概念解释

在阅读和使用这类AI辅助写作工具时，有几个非常核心的概念需要了解：

**1. Preset (预设)**
* **解释：** 指的是预先配置好的一套AI指令或提示词（Prompt）模板。你不需要自己去思考如何给AI下达复杂的指令，只需点击使用这个“预设”，AI就会按照设计好的特定流程（比如这里的三轮问答）来协助你。

**2. Niche (细分领域 / 小众定位)**
* **解释：** 在文学和创作中，指的是非常具体、有针对性的故事子类别。比如，“奇幻（Fantasy）”是一个大类型，而“蒸汽朋克背景下的温馨侦探奇幻（Cozy steampunk detective fantasy）”就是一个 Niche。定位越准确，越容易吸引到特定的目标读者。

**3. Prose style (行文风格 / 笔调)**
* **解释：** 指作者遣词造句的风格、节奏和语气。不同的细分类型需要不同的行文风格。例如，写“硬核科幻”时，行文往往严谨、注重逻辑和技术细节；而写“童话奇幻”时，行文则需要充满诗意和梦幻感。确定了具体的类型，行文风格也就有了锚点。

**4. Codex entry (设定集条目 / 法典条目)**
* **解释：** 常见于 AI 写作软件（如 NovelCrafter 等）中的功能。Codex（法典/设定集）相当于作者的“故事圣经”，用来存放人物、世界观、地点和类型设定。“Codex entry”就是里面的一条具体记录。把 AI 生成的类型特征存入这里，可以让 AI 在后续帮你写正文时，始终参考这些设定，保持风格不跑偏。

**5. Global context (全局上下文)**
* **解释：** 在 AI 写作工具中，AI 能够“记住”的信息。所谓“全局”，意味着只要这条设定存在，无论你当前在写故事的哪一章，AI 都会始终把它放在脑海里作为背景参考，不需要你每次提问时都重复输入一遍。
