推荐安装的插件

- Editing toolbar
Editing Toolbar 是一款高度仿照 Microsoft Word 工具栏设计的 Obsidian 插件

- Clipper
This plugin allows you to capture highlights from the web as Markdown  
entries in Obsidian.


- **Omnisearch**
is a search engine that "_just works_".  
It always instantly shows you the most relevant results, thanks to its smart weighting algorithm.


- Smart Connections
  
Obsidian Smart Connections UPDATE 📝 Rediscover Notes With Obsidian: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7Rvl9Sl29Jk

https://smartconnections.app/smart-connections/list-feature/?utm_source=milestones#core-interactions

- Gemini Scribe for Obsidian

Gemini Scribe is an Obsidian plugin that integrates Google's Gemini AI models, providing powerful AI-driven assistance for note-taking, writing, and knowledge management directly within Obsidian. It leverages your notes as context for AI interactions, making it a highly personalized and integrated experience.
https://effortlessacademic.com/obsidian-ai-agent-with-the-google-gemini-scribe-plugin-for-academics/
